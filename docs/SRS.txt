1.1. Purpose
This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements of Vizura, a cloud-based business operations platform for small New Zealand (NZ) businesses. Following the layout of  Enabel (2025), this document will primarily be used by the Kija development team, any assessors, and any stakeholders interested in reviewing this project.

1.2. Project Scope
Vizura is a web-based platform that combines four key operational functions, consisting of sales analytics and forecasting, rostering, payroll, and employee management, into a singular system. Its initial development will be focused on small retail/hospitality businesses in NZ. Vizura, however, was not designed to replace full-scale accounting software (i.e. Xero), nor will it contain point-of-sale. inventory management, or customer relationship management functionality.

1.2.1. In Scope
For the MVP, Vizura will provide the following key components:
User Management: Registration, authentication, and role-based profile management.
Sales Data: CSV/Excel Upload option and manual entry with basic data cleaning.
Analytics and Forecasting: Dashboards showing trend visualisations.
NLQ Interface: A layer that allows plain English inquiries and responses for historical sales data.
Payroll: Automated pay calculations, HA2003 and ERA2000 calculations, and PDF payslip Generations.
Rostering: Drag and drop scheduling with availability checking, shift swapping, and leave requests.
Employee Management: Employee records, contracts, leave/swap requests, and personal dashboards.
Deployment: Vizura will be hosted on AWS.
Localisation: NZ English will be the default language, as well as NZ date/time zone, and NZ public holidays.

1.2.2. Out of Scope
For the MVP, the following will be excluded but possibly be considered in further iterations:
Full accounting functionality (Like Xero)
Point-of-sale.
Inventory Management.
Customer Relationship Management.
Physical hardware hosting (Cloud hosted for now)
Broad/open-ended NLQ.
Mobile Applications (The MVP will initially be web-based)
Offline capabilities.

2. OVERALL DESCRIPTION

2.1. User Needs
Small NZ business owners currently face the difficulties of using multiple systems to handle their admin load. Multiple tabs, subscriptions, and a lack of data transfer can stretch the resources thinner than needed, and with this approach, these users can expect inefficiency and mistakes to occur commonly. Having all these tasks simplified into a consolidated and affordable source would allow these users to regain some of those crucial resources. 

2.2. Assumptions
Users will be able to access Vizura through up-to-date web browsers .
Users will have and maintain a stable internet connection.
Owners/admins will provide a minimum historical set of sales data.
The AI and Payroll engines will run as separate microservices, accessible and provisioned.
Accurate payroll information will be supplied for the calculations.

2.3. Dependencies
The availability of accurate and up-to-date legislation for payroll compliance.
Sufficient quantity and quality of uploaded sales data for accuracy and forecasting.
The continuous availability of AWS hosting and a reliable internet connection for users/backend services.
